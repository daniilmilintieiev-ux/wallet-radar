# Acceptable sources

Acceptable (must publish the wallet address and allow t0 to be checked on chain): published investigations by on-chain investigators; post-mortems by security firms; incident notices by exchanges and custodians; official statements by projects; court or regulatory documents.

Not acceptable: posts without addresses, rumors, paid promotion, the radar's own output, and claims that the source itself says cannot be established from on-chain flows alone (record as allegation).

Every source URL and date are recorded; the source must be reachable without a login.
